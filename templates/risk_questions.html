🎯 Risk Assessment Questionnaire
Questions staff answer, and the score behind each answer. Staff only see the answers — the scores appear on the risk report. Changes apply to new assessments only.
Score 1 = Low · 2 = Medium · 3 = High. The overall score is the average of the counted answers (≤1.00 Low, 1.01–2.00 Medium, ≥2.01 High).
Not counted — answer is left out of the average (e.g. "Not Applicable"). ⚠ Force High — choosing this answer makes the whole assessment High Risk. High with top volume — High Risk when combined with the highest "Expected volume" answer (e.g. Cash).
Country list questions use the scores in Country Risk Scores (countries marked "Force High" there, e.g. Iran, make the assessment High Risk).
